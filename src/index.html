About Cognitive Evolution News

This project aggregates the latest research and discoveries in early human cognition, archaeological findings, and paleoanthropology. We pull from multiple sources including arXiv, ScienceDaily, SAPIENS, and HeritageDaily to bring you the most relevant and up-to-date information on human cognitive evolution.

Our focus areas include cognitive studies, fossil discoveries, ancient tools and technology, symbolic art, genetic research, and behavioral studies of early human societies.

Sources: arXiv, ScienceDaily, SAPIENS, HeritageDaily, PLOS ONE, and more.
