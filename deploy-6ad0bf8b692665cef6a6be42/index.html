This archive follows early human cognitive evolution and the archaeology, paleoanthropology, and anthropology that help explain it. We collect source summaries and scholarly metadata, preserve links to original publications, and keep older discoveries available.

Direct matches enter the archive automatically. Broader archaeology goes to an editorial review queue. Off-topic results are excluded. We preserve abstracts and permitted summaries, not full publications; all original work belongs to its authors and publishers.
